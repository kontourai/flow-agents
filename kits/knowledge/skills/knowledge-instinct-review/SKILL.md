# Instinct review

Review the claim, source observations, scope, confidence and any proposed retirement before deciding. A reviewer must be different from the proposer. A tool-derived claim is not eligible for a brief until its raw observation and the claim have been independently applied; a rejected or retired source cannot authorize it.

Keep legacy data and source observations. Do not delete records, alter a schedule, install skills, or archive a store as part of review. Use the owner's retirement plan only after the migration and retrieval checks are complete.
